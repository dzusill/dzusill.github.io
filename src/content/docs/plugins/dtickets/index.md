A ticket and player-report desk for Paper, built on **DzusillCore**.

A complaint typed into chat is gone the moment the next line scrolls past. dTickets turns it into a
record with a category, a description, evidence and an owner — and gives your staff one screen where
nothing sits forgotten at the bottom.

- 🧭 **A guided wizard, not a text box.** Players pick a category, answer its questions and attach
  what they saw. Every ticket arrives complete, so staff never chase someone for details.
- 💬 **The conversation lives inside the ticket.** Replies stay attached to the case. Hand it over and
  the next staffer reads the whole story in seconds.
- 🚩 **Reports open with the accused already attached** — name, location, nearby players, the last
  fifteen lines of chat, and any anticheat flags from the window around the report.
- ⚖️ **Punish and close in one motion.** A configurable action list runs your own commands, with the
  ticket number in the reason.
- 📋 **A staff queue that sorts itself.** Priority, status, stale and late flags, unclaimed markers,
  and a merge counter when several people report the same thing.
- 🙈 **Vanish-aware everywhere.** PremiumVanish and SuperVanish levels are respected in lists, counts,
  alerts and tab completion.
- 🔔 **Per-staffer notification preferences**, per event, with sounds and optional titles.
- 📊 **Response-time statistics** so you can promote staff on numbers instead of on vibes.

Everything a player or a staffer sees — categories, wizard questions, menus, sounds, colours and every
message — is plain YAML you own.
